Post-Flood Recovery — First 24 Hours

For Harris County, TX residents • Auto-localized

Document everything before cleanup
Photo/video every room before moving anything. Required for FEMA + insurance.
Cut power if water is near outlets
Only re-enter after utility company confirms safe. Don't gamble here.
Begin water extraction within 24hrs
Mold growth starts at hour 48. Wet vac → dehumidifier → air movers.
File initial FEMA claim (deadline: 60 days)
DisasterAssistance.gov. Most claims approved are filed in first 7 days.
Treat porous materials with mold control
Drywall, insulation, carpet underlayment — usually replace, don't restore.
